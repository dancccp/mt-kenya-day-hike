About the hike

The Sirimon route is one of the most scenic ways onto Mt Kenya. The path climbs gently out of the forest into open moorland, where giant lobelias and groundsels line the trail and the jagged peaks slowly come into view.

This is a long, demanding day at high altitude, and it’s a day hike, not a summit climb. How far you go depends on your fitness, the weather and your guides’ call. We split the group by pace so nobody is rushed or left behind, and anyone can turn back when their body says it’s time.

How the day runs

  • 4:00 amMeet in Nairobi at Kencom, opposite International House. If you’re close to Thika Road, we can pick you up on request, though meeting at Kencom is preferred.
  • 4:30 amThe bus leaves. We leave on time, so be there early.
  • 7:30 amArrive at Mt Kenya. We register with the park, get a safety briefing and split into pace groups.
  • Morning to afternoonHike from Old Moses with your guides. The lead guide sets the turn-back time on the day, depending on pace and weather.
  • EveningBack on the bus to Nairobi. We share the expected arrival time in the group chat.

What to bring

  • Sturdy hiking shoes
  • A rain jacket
  • Warm layers, gloves and a hat
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • A small backpack
  • At least 2 liters of water and high-energy snacks
  • Any personal medication
  • ID or passport

Cancellation policy

Plans change, so here is how we handle it.

  • Cancel 7 or more days before the hike: full refund.
  • Cancel less than 7 days before: your deposit isn’t refunded, but you can pass your slot to a friend. Just send us their name and details.
  • No-show or late arrival: no refund. The bus leaves at 4:30 am.

We may decline anyone who isn’t fit to hike or who puts the group at risk.
